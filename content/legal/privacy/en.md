This policy explains which personal data **GBC Foods**, the food division of GBC Group (“GBC”, “we”), processes when you visit this site, request a quote or contact us, and what your rights are. It is written to comply with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018 — LGPD) and, for visitors from the European Union, with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 1. Who the controller is

GBC Foods / GBC Group, with head office in Santos, São Paulo, Brazil. Channel for privacy matters and for the data protection officer: **commercial@gbc-foods.com**, subject “Privacy”.

## 2. What data we process and why

**Quote requests and contact.** When you use the quote form or the WhatsApp button, you send us your name, company, e-mail, phone, country, product of interest, volume, Incoterm, destination port, shipment window, payment terms and the message you write. We use this data to answer your request, prepare samples and proposals and, if a deal follows, to perform the contract. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps and performance of a contract (LGPD art. 7, V; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) and, for sending the form, your consent (LGPD art. 7, I; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

The message is sent through **WhatsApp**, a service of Meta Platforms, and is also subject to WhatsApp's privacy policy. A copy of the form data is stored in our commercial system, hosted on the Supabase platform, so the team can follow up on the request.

**Usage statistics.** With your permission in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to learn which pages are visited, from which country and on which device. Without permission, no analytics cookie is set. Legal basis: consent (LGPD art. 7, I; GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). Details in the [Cookie Policy]({cookies_url}).

**Technical logs.** The hosting provider (Cloudflare) records the IP address, browser and time of each access, for security and to keep the site running. Legal basis: legitimate interest (LGPD art. 7, IX; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)), including fraud prevention.

We do not process sensitive data and we make no automated decisions with effects on you. This site is addressed to businesses; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## 3. Who we share data with

- **Meta Platforms (WhatsApp)** — delivery of the quote or contact message.
- **Supabase** — hosting of the commercial system's database.
- **Google (Google Analytics)** — usage statistics, only with your consent.
- **Cloudflare** — hosting and security of the site.
- Carriers, customs brokers, banks and insurers, where needed to perform a contract you enter into with us.
- Authorities, where required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. International transfers

Some of these providers keep servers outside Brazil (United States and European Union). Transfers rely on contractual clauses and on the safeguards each provider offers, under LGPD art. 33 and GDPR chapter V.

## 5. How long we keep data

Quote and contact data: for as long as there is a commercial relationship or a legitimate interest in keeping it, and up to five years after the last contact, unless a legal duty requires longer retention (tax and export documents). Statistics data: fourteen months, within Google Analytics. Technical logs: up to six months.

## 6. Your rights

At any time you may ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymisation, portability or deletion of your data, information on whom we share it with, and withdraw consent (LGPD art. 18). Residents of the European Union have the equivalent GDPR rights, including the right to object. Write to **commercial@gbc-foods.com** with the subject “Privacy”; we reply within fifteen days. You may also lodge a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or with the authority of your country.

## 7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, the commercial system requires authentication and access to data is restricted to the team that needs it. No system is infallible; if an incident affects your data, we will notify you and the ANPD as the law requires.

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date of the current version appears at the top of the page; material changes will be highlighted on the site.
